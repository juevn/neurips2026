How this was made

  • Papers and institutions come from the official neurips.cc program data, which lists each author's institution from their OpenReview profile. Main track only.
  • Counting: a paper counts once for every institution with at least one author on it, so shares add up to more than 100%. The fractional count splits each paper equally across its authors, then across each author's institutions.
  • Ranks are by paper count; institutions with the same count share a rank.
  • Names are normalized with a hand-checked table of about 380 institutions. Google includes DeepMind, Meta includes FAIR, all Chinese Academy of Sciences institutes are merged, HKUST and HKUST (Guangzhou) are separate. Less common names are grouped by their exact spelling, so a small institution may appear under more than one name.
  • Research areas are not official tags. Titles and abstracts were embedded (bge-small) and clustered (k-means), then the clusters were named from their top terms. Each paper has exactly one area, and the borders between neighbouring areas are fuzzy. For institutions with only a few papers, the mix is noisy.
  • Industry vs academia: companies and corporate labs are industry. Universities and public or non-profit institutes such as CAS, Max Planck, RIKEN, Shanghai AI Lab and Mila are academia.
  • Only institution-level totals are published here. Download the full table (CSV).
  • Spotted a wrong mapping? Corrections are welcome.
