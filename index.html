How this was made

data, method and caveats
  • Papers and institutions come from the official neurips.cc program data, which lists each author's institution from their OpenReview profile. Main track only, snapshot . Papers come from both the schedule feed and the poster list in Downloads, so posters without a session yet are included. Rankings and institution pages use the main track only; topic search also covers the Evaluations & Datasets and Position tracks.
  • Counting: a paper counts once for every institution with at least one author on it, so shares add up to more than 100%. The fractional count splits each paper equally across its authors, then across each author's institutions.
  • Names are normalized with a hand-checked table of about 380 institutions plus rules for typos, sub-units and acronyms. Google includes DeepMind, Meta includes FAIR, all Chinese Academy of Sciences institutes are merged, HKUST and HKUST (Guangzhou) are separate. Less common names may still appear under more than one spelling.
  • Research areas are not official tags. Titles and abstracts were embedded with bge-small-en-v1.5 and clustered, then the clusters were named from their top terms. Borders between neighbouring areas are fuzzy.
  • Topic search runs entirely in your browser: your text is embedded with the same model (downloaded once, about 33 MB) and compared with every paper. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
  • Industry vs academia: companies and corporate labs are industry. Universities and public or non-profit institutes such as CAS, Max Planck, RIKEN, Shanghai AI Lab and Mila are academia.
  • No author names are included. Download the institution table (CSV).
  • Spotted a wrong mapping? Corrections are welcome.
